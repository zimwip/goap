# ADR 0059 — The prompt of every LLM call is an entry of the change log

**Status**: accepted, implemented · **Date**: 2026-10 · Builds on ADR 0011, 0020, 0030, 0057. The exchange type moved
to `pkg/journal` (ADR 0066); the Tokens console reads the LLM call ledger (ADR 0089), which keeps the exchange of
every call that is not an engine call of a change.

## Context

The execution journal (ADR 0011) kept, for each LLM call of an action, its provider, model, token counts, duration and
error, and nothing of what was said: the request (`llm.Request`: system text and messages) went to the model gateway and
was dropped, and so was the answer once the action had read it. The Tokens console could say a call cost 12,000 input
tokens but not what those tokens were. The same hole weakens the audit (ADR 0057): a provenance of what an AI did that
cannot show what it was asked.

## Decision

- **A new stream of the log, `model`** (ADR 0030): one `model.call` entry per call, inline, with the request and the
  answer (`journal.ModelExchange`: `step`, `call`, `system`, `messages`, `response`, `truncated`; the behaviours of
  ADR 0093 since). It belongs to the action
  run that made the call (`execution`, `process`, `flow` as its journal record; `subject` is the position of the call
  in the record's `modelCalls`). The journal record keeps the summary only: `ModelCall.Exchange` is carried from the
  engine to `journal.Entries` (through `journal.Append`), which writes the entries next to the record and strips it, so
  a record read back never carries prompts and the observer and the run views do not pay for them.
- **Capped**: each text is cut to `journal.MaxExchangeText` (256 KiB) and the entry flagged `truncated`.
- **The process does not keep them**: the engine forgets the text once the step is journaled (`Step.dropExchanges`);
  `LLMCall.Exchange` is not serialized.
- **Read with their own permission**, `prompt:inspect` on the project of the change (default policy: members of the
  project; administrators always). `ListChangeLog` refuses a request naming `model.` types without it and leaves them
  out of an unfiltered read; the PROV-O export drops them for the same callers. A graph already holding stored policies
  grants it to administrators only until a rule is added.
- **PROV-O** (ADR 0057): an entry is a `goap:ModelExchange` entity generated by the execution, attributed to its agent.
- **Web**: in the Tokens console, the input and output cells open the prompt and the answer
  (`ModelExchangeDialog.svelte`), read lazily from the log by process, step and call. Since ADR 0089 the console lists
  the rows of the ledger; an engine call of a change still reads its exchange here, the other calls from the ledger
  (`GetCallExchange`).

## Consequences

- Prompts can hold anything the project works on, and the log is permanent: the permission above is the control, and
  `MaxExchangeText` bounds the size. Deleting a change (`PurgeChange`) deletes them with its log.
- Only calls of actions are recorded in the change log (planner calls included, as they are folded into the step that
  follows; the other callers of a model are kept by the ledger, ADR 0089). A planning
  call on a path that never reaches a step (blocked board, goal reached, stuck) is counted in its tick record but its
  text is not kept. Calls made before this ADR have no entry; the dialog says so.
- A dedicated store for large prompts (deduplicating the brief repeated at every call) is open if the log grows too fast.
