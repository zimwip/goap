# ADR 0057 — The log of a change exports as W3C PROV-O

**Status**: accepted, implemented · **Date**: 2026-10 · Builds on ADR 0011, 0024, 0029, 0030, 0032, 0056.

## Context

Everything that happens to a change is one log (ADR 0030): its facts, the journal records of its execution and the
events of its change impacts. The Audit pane exports it as CSV or as GOAP's own JSON, which only GOAP reads. Proving
what an AI did, on whose behalf, from what, and who accepted it (traceability and human oversight, as the EU AI Act
and ISO/IEC 42001 ask) calls for a standard, tool-independent form that provenance stores, knowledge graphs and audit
tools already read: W3C PROV-O.

## Decision

- **`pkg/prov`** (no infra deps) builds a JSON-LD document (`application/ld+json`, PROV-O + the `goap:` vocabulary
  `https://github.com/zimwip/goap/ns/prov#`) from a change and its whole log (`prov.Export`). Instances are named
  `urn:goap:<kind>:<id>` (`change`, `execution`, `item`, `impact`, `node`, `baseline`, `unit`, `principal`, `agent`,
  `model`, `tool`, `plan`, `review`, `adoption`).
- **Mapping.**
  - The **change** is a `prov:Activity` (`goap:intent`, methodology, namespace, project, status), associated with its
    holding unit (`prov:Organization`); it `prov:used` the baseline it started from (none for the empty state), and
    the baselines it left (`ResultBaselineID`, every `landed` baseline) were `prov:wasGeneratedBy` it, the result a
    `prov:wasRevisionOf` the start.
  - A **journal record** is a `prov:Activity` `goap:partOf` the change, with its times; it is associated with the
    person deciding a human action, else the methodology agent (`prov:SoftwareAgent`), through a
    `prov:qualifiedAssociation` whose `prov:hadPlan` is the methodology action it ran (`<methodology>@<version>#<action>`);
    the models and tools it called are associated software agents; it `prov:used` the node versions it read.
  - A **fact** is a `prov:Entity` generated by its action run (else the change), attributed to its producer, derived
    from the items it came from, a revision of the ones it supersedes; a decision names the item it decides.
  - A **change impact** is a `prov:Entity` (key, type, intent, rationale) derived from its items. A written version is
    a `prov:Entity` `prov:specializationOf` its node, generated by its run, attributed to its caller, a
    `prov:wasRevisionOf` its pre version (the rebased one when it was rebased). A review is a `prov:Activity` of its
    reviewer using the version written. A landed version is a `prov:hadMember` of the baseline (a `prov:Collection`) it
    landed in; a merge version (ADR 0032) derives from the version written. An adopted flow is an activity that
    `goap:replaces` the runs it made stale.
- **Nothing lost.** Every described entry carries its log position (`goap:seq`), type and flow, and its whole record
  (`goap:record`, a JSON literal): what PROV does not model stays readable.
- **Served** by `GraphService.ExportChangeProvenance` (the whole log, whatever the filters; personal changes stay
  their owner's through `PersonalScope`, as every request naming a `change_id`); the Audit pane's "Export PROV-O"
  downloads it.

## Consequences

- An audit or a provenance store can follow an AI's output from the model call to the node version that landed, with
  the person who accepted it, without knowing GOAP.
- The mapping is a projection of the log: the log stays the truth, the export is recomputed on demand.
- Not done: Turtle / PROV-N serializations, a PROV export of a whole project or time range, signing the document.
